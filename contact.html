Getting here

How to find us

We're in Caranzalem, a few minutes from Panaji. Easy to reach by car, scooter, or cab.

01

By car or scooter

Head toward Caranzalem Beach Road. Look for the TSF signage on the building — parking is available along the street.

02

By cab

Any Goan taxi or Ola/Uber will know Caranzalem. Ask for the Martins Landscape building — we're right beside it.

03

By bus

Take any bus toward Panaji. From the Caranzalem stop, it's a short walk to the studio.

Common questions

Before you visit

A few things people usually ask before their first session. Anything else — just message us.

Do I need to book a class in advance?

No. Walk in during opening hours and we'll get you into the next available class. If you'd rather plan ahead, message us on WhatsApp and we'll reserve a spot.

Is the first class really free?

Yes. First session is on us — no card, no commitment. Come in, try the class, see how you feel. If it's a fit, we'll talk about membership afterwards.

I've never trained before. Can I still join?

Absolutely. Most of our students started as complete beginners. Every program has an on-ramp for first-timers, and coaches work with you at your pace.

What should I bring to my first class?

Comfortable workout clothes and a water bottle. For combat sports, we'll provide any gear you need for the first session. Bring indoor shoes if you have them.

Do you have programs for kids?

Yes — six programs for kids including Taekwondo, Kickboxing, Yoga, Gymnastics, Calisthenics, and Dance. Ages and schedules vary. Check the programs page for details.
