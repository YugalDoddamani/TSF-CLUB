The founder

Master Suresh

Founder · Head Coach · Caranzalem, Goa

Master Suresh has spent over a decade teaching martial arts and fitness in Goa. A second-degree black belt in Taekwondo, he founded TSF Fitness Studio in 2018, building it around a simple idea — that skill is earned through repetition, not shortcuts.

Train with Suresh

The story

Ten years on the mat

Suresh began training in Taekwondo as a young man and never really stopped. What started as a discipline became a practice, then a career. Over the years he's competed, officiated, and coached — building the kind of experience that only comes from time on the floor.

His approach has stayed consistent through all of it. Technique comes before intensity. A student learns the movement, drills it until it becomes second nature, and only then adds speed, power, and pressure. It's a slower path at the beginning — and it's the reason his students stay.

You don't build fighters with shortcuts. You build them with years.

Beyond the studio, Suresh serves as President of STAG — the SaJa Taekwondo Association of Goa — and as Head Coach of Goa under the IITF. He has worked with athletes across age groups, from young beginners finding their footing to fighters preparing for state-level competition.

In 2018, he opened TSF as a place where combat sports, strength, and conditioning could live under one roof. Today the studio trains adults and kids across six disciplines — MMA, Taekwondo, CrossFit, Calisthenics, Yoga, and Dance — with a coaching philosophy that hasn't changed since the first class.

The approach

How he teaches

Three principles run through every class, every session, every student.

01

Technique before intensity

Speed and power mean nothing without control. Fundamentals come first — always.

02

Discipline before everything

Show up. Do the work. The rest — fitness, confidence, skill — follows on its own.

03

Progress over perfection

Nobody arrives ready. The point is to keep moving forward, one session at a time.
