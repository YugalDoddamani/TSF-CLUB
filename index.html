The founder

Master Suresh

Master Suresh is a certified personal trainer and martial arts coach with a second-degree black belt in Taekwondo. He is President of STAG, the SaJa Taekwondo Association of Goa, and Head Coach of Goa under IITF.

You don't build fighters with shortcuts. You build them with years.

With ten years of teaching experience across six disciplines, his approach hasn't changed: technique before intensity, discipline before everything.
