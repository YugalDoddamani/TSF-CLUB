Privacy
policy.

A straightforward explanation of what information may be handled when you visit the TSF Fitness Studio public website, use its tools, or contact the studio.

Scope matters. This page covers the public website at tsfclub.com. If TSF launches a member portal that stores account, membership, payment, attendance, or health information, a separate member-app privacy notice must be provided before that information is collected.

01

Who operates this website?

The website is operated by TSF Fitness Studio (Team Suresh Fitness), Caranzalem, Goa, India.

02

Information collected by the public website

The public site is primarily informational. It does not currently provide a general account registration or sign-in form.

  • Information you send directly. If you contact TSF by email, telephone, or WhatsApp, the studio and the service you use will receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, contact details, and message.
  • Macro calculator. Calculator values are intended to be processed in your browser and are not submitted through a TSF account form. This behaviour should be re-verified whenever the calculator code changes.
  • Payment page. The public payment page generates UPI payment details or a QR code. A payment is completed in the UPI app you choose. Do not treat this page as a member account or assume that it confirms a payment to TSF automatically.
  • Technical information. Hosting, security, and network services may process standard request information such as IP address, browser details, and requested URLs to deliver and protect the website. TSF does not use this policy to claim that infrastructure providers never process such information.
03

Cookies and browser storage

The site saves a colour-theme preference in browser local storage so the selected appearance can persist between visits. Local storage is browser storage and is not itself a cookie.

The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts and embeds a Google Maps map on the contact page. Those third-party services may receive technical information, including your IP address, when your browser requests their resources. Their own policies apply to their processing.

You can clear local storage and cookies through your browser settings. Blocking third-party resources may affect how some parts of the site look or work.

04

Why information is used

Information provided in a direct message is used to respond to the enquiry, discuss classes or memberships, and provide the service you asked about. Technical request information may be used by hosting and security providers to operate, diagnose, and protect the website.

TSF does not intend to sell personal information collected through the public website. Do not send medical records, payment credentials, passwords, or other highly sensitive information through a general enquiry channel.

05

Third-party services

The public website currently uses or links to the following third-party services:

  • Google — Google Fonts and the embedded map on the contact page.
  • GitHub — website source hosting and, depending on deployment configuration, static website hosting.
  • External communication and payment services you choose to use, such as WhatsApp, email, and your UPI application. Those services process information under their own terms and privacy notices.

The exact hosting provider and any added analytics, anti-spam, payment, or messaging integrations should be reviewed before each major release. This notice must be updated if the implementation changes.

06

Retention and security

Messages sent through external channels may remain in those services and in TSF’s correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage the relationship, maintain business records, or address a dispute. Retention may also be required by applicable law.

TSF takes reasonable steps to maintain the public website and limit unnecessary access to information. No website or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

07

Children

TSF offers training programs for children. The public website is informational and should not be used to submit a child’s sensitive information. Where information about a child is needed for membership or training administration, TSF should provide an appropriate parent or guardian notice and obtain any consent required by applicable law before collecting it.

08

Your choices and requests

You can choose what information to include when contacting the studio, manage cookies and local storage in your browser, and contact TSF to ask about information you have sent directly.

09

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated when the website, its providers, or applicable requirements change. The date at the top indicates the version currently published on this page.

Review before launch. Confirm the effective date, legal operating entity, hosting provider, third-party scripts, and contact details. This draft is a practical website notice, not a legal opinion or a certification of compliance.
