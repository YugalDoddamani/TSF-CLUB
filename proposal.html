01 — Overview

A single platform for the studio, the members, and the coaches.

The TSF Digital Platform replaces the current register, the payment screenshots on WhatsApp, and the manual fee tracking with a clean, connected system. Everything lives under one domain. Everything is owned by the studio. Nothing is rented from a third-party gym software.

The platform has three parts — all sharing the same design, the same brand, and the same login:

Public site

tsfclub.com

The marketing and information site. Programs, founder, contact, macro calculator, payment page. Already live and maintained as part of this engagement.

Member & Admin app

members.tsfclub.com

The new platform. Members register, pay fees, view their program, and get notifications. Admins and coaches log in to the same app with different levels of access.

Everything is owned by the studio. The code, the database, the accounts, the domain, the payment gateway, the phone number — all registered in the studio's name. There is no vendor lock-in, no monthly licence, and no third-party app standing between TSF and its members.

02 — Member App

What every member sees.

Members register with their Gmail, and from that point on, everything they need is one tap away — fees, program details, events, and their own training history.

Registration & Onboarding

  • Gmail sign-in. One tap, no password to remember. Secure and verified.
  • WhatsApp number collection at signup — used for all future notifications.
  • Program selection from a live list of all active programs at the studio.
  • Preferred joining date picked from a date selector, so members can start on a day that suits them.
  • Emergency contact and basic health notes captured once and stored securely.
  • Welcome flow that confirms their registration and shows the next steps.

Member Dashboard

  • Profile card — name, photo, program, coach, and contact details.
  • Program status — which program they are enrolled in, how long they have been running it, and days remaining.
  • Fee status — current month's fee, due date, payment status, and a single button to pay.
  • Streak counter — consecutive sessions attended, updated by the coach.
  • Payment history — every payment made, with downloadable receipts.
  • Upcoming events — events the studio has published, with a one-tap "I'm interested" button.
  • Notification centre — a log of all WhatsApp messages and emails sent to them.

Payments

  • Pay Now button opens a secure Razorpay checkout in the member's app.
  • All payment modes — UPI, cards, net banking, wallets, and EMI for larger amounts.
  • Instant receipt delivered by email and WhatsApp the moment payment succeeds.
  • Auto-pay option — members can authorize a recurring UPI mandate, and the fee is deducted automatically each month. Nothing to remember, nothing to miss.
  • Payment records stored permanently against the member's profile.
03 — Admin App

What the studio sees.

A complete back office, accessed from any device. The owner logs in from their phone, their laptop, or the receptionist's computer — and sees the same data, always up to date.

Member Management

  • Full member list with search by name, phone, program, or membership status.
  • Add a member manually — for walk-ins or members who prefer to register in person.
  • Edit any field — program, joining date, amount due, streak count, notes.
  • Deactivate or archive — with a record of when and why.
  • Private notes on each member — injuries, preferences, or anything worth remembering.
  • CSV export for accounting, backups, or offline review.

Fee & Payment Management

  • Overview of all dues — who has paid, who is pending, who is overdue.
  • Filters by due date, program, member, or payment status.
  • Payment records from Razorpay synced into the admin dashboard in real time.
  • Manual entry for cash payments made at the reception.
  • Export to Excel for month-end reconciliation.

Events Management

  • Create an event — name, date, venue, description, image, capacity.
  • Publish with one tap. The moment an event is published, every active member receives a WhatsApp notification.
  • Interest tracking — see who has tapped "I'm interested" so you can plan attendance.
  • Reminder broadcast — send a follow-up message 24 hours before the event.
  • Archive past events with photos and a short write-up.

Reports & Insights

  • Monthly revenue — collected, pending, and projected.
  • Member growth — new signups, active members, and inactive members.
  • Program breakdown — how many members in each program.
  • Dues report — upcoming payments and overdue accounts, at a glance.
04 — Coach Access

Coaches get exactly what they need. Nothing more.

The platform has a built-in role system. When a new account is created, the owner assigns a role — Admin, Coach, or Member. Each role determines what that person can see and do.

Feature Admin (Owner) Coach
View member list Full access Only members of their assigned programs
Member personal details (phone, email, emergency contact) Full access Not visible
Payment records Full access Not visible
Fee reminders & billing Full access Not visible
Attendance / streak updates Full access Only for their assigned programs
Create events Yes Not permitted
Reports Full access Not visible

Coaches use the same member app — they simply log in with their assigned role. This keeps the platform simple for everyone, and ensures that member privacy is protected without adding extra tools.

05 — Communication

WhatsApp and email — the studio's voice, on autopilot.

Every important moment in a member's journey triggers the right message, automatically. No one has to remember to send anything.

WhatsApp Business API

The studio's own verified WhatsApp Business number sends all member communication. Messages come from the official business account, not from a personal number, so they carry authority and stay reliable.

  • Welcome message — sent the moment a member registers.
  • Fee reminder — sent 5 days before the due date, again 1 day before, and on the due date.
  • Overdue reminder — sent if a fee is not paid on time, with a direct link to pay.
  • Payment confirmation — sent immediately after a successful payment, with the receipt attached.
  • Event notification — sent the moment the studio publishes a new event.
  • Event reminder — sent 24 hours before an event the member showed interest in.
  • Membership expiry reminder — sent one week before a program ends, to encourage renewal.

Email

A dedicated email address on the studio's own domain handles all formal correspondence. This replaces the current reliance on a Gmail address and gives TSF a professional, branded touch.

  • Sender address — messages come from hello@tsfclub.com or a similar address on the studio's domain.
  • Payment receipts — formal invoices generated automatically for every payment.
  • Welcome email — with program details, coach introduction, and what to expect on day one.
  • Monthly summary — optional monthly email to the owner with revenue, growth, and dues.

Message costs. WhatsApp utility messages in India cost approximately ₹0.11 to ₹0.35 each. Based on a studio of 200 members receiving 3–4 messages per month, the total WhatsApp cost is roughly ₹200 to ₹400 per month — a small, predictable monthly cost that scales with the member base, not a fixed subscription.

06 — Payment System

Razorpay — India's leading payment gateway, in the studio's name.

All payments — membership fees, event entry, personal training, or custom amounts — flow through a single Razorpay account registered under the studio's business name.

What the payment system handles

  • Membership fees — monthly, quarterly, or yearly programs.
  • Event registrations — one-time payments for fight nights, boot camps, or workshops.
  • Personal training — session-based or package-based payments.
  • Custom amounts — for anything the studio needs to collect outside the standard catalog.
  • Recurring payments — via UPI Autopay mandate, so monthly fees are collected without members needing to remember.

Transaction fees — what they cover

Razorpay charges a small fee on each successful transaction, deducted at source. This is not a fee paid to the developer; it is what the payment network charges for delivering money securely from the member to the studio's bank account.

Payment method Transaction fee What it covers
UPI Approximately 2% + GST Instant bank-to-bank transfer, auto-receipt generation, and settlement into the studio's account within T+2 days
Credit / Debit card Approximately 2% + GST Full card processing, PCI-DSS compliance, fraud detection, and settlement
Net banking Approximately 2% + GST Secure bank authentication and instant confirmation
Wallets Approximately 2% + GST Paytm, PhonePe, Amazon Pay, and other wallet integrations
Recurring (UPI Autopay) Approximately 1.5% + GST Automatic monthly collection, mandate management, and failure retries

The benefits of these fees. Every rupee of the transaction fee buys real value — instant payments, fraud protection, automatic receipts, dispute resolution, settlement reports for accounting, and round-the-clock support. This is what allows the studio to move away from personal UPI numbers and screenshots toward a formal, auditable payment system.

07 — Privacy & Compliance

Member data is protected by design.

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act requires businesses that handle personal information to follow specific practices. The platform is built to meet those standards from day one.

  • Privacy policy page — a clear, plain-English document explaining what data is collected, why, how it is stored, and how members can request its removal.
  • Terms of service page — covering membership, fees, refunds, and acceptable use of the platform.
  • Data encryption — all member data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Row-level security — a member can only view their own data. A coach can only view members of their programs. An admin can view everything.
  • Consent tracking — members explicitly consent to receiving WhatsApp notifications during signup.
  • Data portability — the studio can export the entire member database at any time in standard formats.
  • Right to deletion — members can request account deletion, and the platform supports it cleanly.
08 — Ownership

What the studio owns at the end of the build.

There is no vendor lock-in, no rented platform, and no monthly software licence. Everything created in this engagement belongs to Team Suresh Fitness, permanently.

  • The domain name — tsfclub.com, already registered in the studio's name.
  • The codebase — every line of HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and backend configuration. Full source access.
  • The database — all member records, payment history, event data, and notification logs, stored in the studio's own account.
  • The Razorpay account — registered to the studio's business PAN and bank account. Money lands directly in the studio's account, never in the developer's.
  • The WhatsApp Business number — verified under the studio's name. Members see the studio as the sender.
  • The email address on the domain — hello@tsfclub.com or similar, owned by the studio.
  • The admin access — top-level credentials to every service used, held by the studio owner.
  • The right to migrate — the studio can move the platform to any hosting provider at any time, with full data export support.

Compare this to off-the-shelf gym software. Most gym software charges a monthly subscription, holds the member data in its own systems, and offers no real ownership. Over three years, that subscription typically exceeds the one-time cost of a custom build, and at the end of it the studio still owns nothing. This engagement inverts that model entirely.
